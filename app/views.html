Training labels per product

how many confirmed examples exist, and whether that is enough to fit a model
A model needs roughly 10 confirmed examples per signal it weighs. These products weigh 23–27 drivers each, so the bar is about 270 positives. Where the direct Salesforce label is too rare, the model falls back to the category label — "this company bought something in this category, Salesforce or a rival". Commerce Cloud is detectable on 0.2% of domains but the commerce category is on 6.2%, which is the difference between needing 292,000 probes and needing 8,100. Where neither clears the bar, the model declines to fit rather than reporting a number built on noise.
